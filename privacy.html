Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This policy explains how information is handled within the NOVA Bank educational demonstration.

1. Information Collection

The application may collect sample information entered into its forms for demonstration purposes. No real banking identity verification is performed.

2. Browser Storage

Some demo preferences, beneficiary details and transaction records may be stored locally using browser Local Storage or Session Storage.

3. Data Security

This front-end project does not provide production-grade encryption, secure authentication or server-side data protection. Do not enter real passwords or sensitive financial information.

4. Third-Party Services

The current demonstration does not process banking information through external financial institutions or payment providers.

5. Data Removal

Demo information stored in the browser can be cleared through the browser's site data settings. Clearing browser storage may remove saved demo preferences and records.

6. Policy Updates

This policy may be revised as the educational project develops and new features are introduced.
