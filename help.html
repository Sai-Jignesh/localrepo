Frequently Asked Questions

How can I access my dashboard?

Open the login page and use the demo interface to explore the banking dashboard.

How can I transfer money?

Open the Transfer Money section, enter the recipient details and amount, and continue to the demo confirmation page.

Can I view my transaction history?

Yes. The Transaction History section lets you search, filter, sort and export sample transactions.

How do I manage beneficiaries?

Visit Manage Beneficiaries to add, search, remove and export saved demo beneficiaries.

Can I change notification preferences?

Yes. Open Settings to update your notification preferences in the browser demo.

Are real banking transactions supported?

No. NOVA Bank is an educational front-end demonstration. It does not process real payments or connect to a banking server.
